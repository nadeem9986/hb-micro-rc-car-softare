Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2024-08-15

This app controls a micro RC car over Bluetooth Low Energy.

The app requests Bluetooth permission to scan for and connect to nearby car hardware. On Android, Location/GPS may be required by the operating system for BLE scanning, but this app does not read, store, or share your location.

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information.

If you deny Bluetooth or Location permission, the app may not be able to discover or control the car.
